How to play

Game guide

New players start with the 60-Second Bond Trial: collect the ionic, covalent and metallic motes, forge your first bond, and unlock combat before entering the Chem Lab.

Open Chem Lab to continue the next chemistry challenge. Correct steps earn 5 XP + 3 gold; a perfect challenge adds 30 XP + 20 gold + 1 crystal. A 3+ answer streak earns ×1.5.

Use Daily for a 3-question set with double rewards. Open a companion draw from the HUD ✦ button or the party menu after every 2 challenges.

In the world, use WASD to move, the mouse to look and E to interact. On touchscreens, use the left stick, drag to look and tap Interact.

With combat enabled, left-click or press J to attack, K for a skill and B for the gear shop.

    Chemical bonding review

    Ionic · NaCl Electron transfer forms Na⁺ and Cl⁻. Oppositely charged ions attract throughout a giant lattice. NaCl gives their ratio.

    Covalent · H₂ and diamond Atoms share electron pairs. H₂ forms separate molecules. Diamond forms a giant covalent network.

    Metallic · Fe Positive metal ions attract delocalised electrons throughout the metal lattice.
